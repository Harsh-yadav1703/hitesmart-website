About HireSmart

Better connections. Better careers.

HireSmart is a student web-development project concept designed to make recruitment easier for candidates and HR teams. It demonstrates how a simple digital platform can organize job discovery and candidate applications.

✓ Easy-to-use job search interface
✓ Simple candidate application form
✓ Clear recruitment information
